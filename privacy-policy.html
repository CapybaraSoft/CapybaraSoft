Privacy Policy — UV Index + Sun Widget

Effective date: September 17, 2026

Quick summary
• This free app shows the UV index and displays banner ads via Google AdMob.
• No account is required. We do not run our own servers for this app.
• The app does not use Firebase Analytics or another app-usage analytics service.
• Device location and chosen cities are stored only on your device so the app and widget can remember your location.
• Advertising is provided by Google AdMob, which may collect device and ad identifiers to deliver and measure ads.
• Forecasts come from MET Norway. Coordinates are deliberately rounded to about 1 km before they are sent, to obscure your exact location.
• For users in the EEA/UK/Switzerland, we show a consent form (Google’s UMP). You can change your choice any time from the app’s Privacy options.

What this app does

UV Index + Sun Widget provides current UV index information and related guidance. The app is free to use and supported by advertising.

Information we collect

1) Information you provide

We don’t require you to create an account or submit personal information to use the app.

2) Information collected automatically through advertising

We use Google AdMob to show ads. When ads load, Google and its partners may collect information such as the Advertising ID (AAID), IP address and coarse location, device/app information, and ad interaction data. This is used to deliver, limit, and measure ads; detect fraud/abuse; and (where permitted) personalize ads. Learn more from Google: policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

The app does not use Firebase Analytics or another app-usage analytics service. We do not send screen views, feature usage, selected cities, location-permission status, or precise coordinates to Firebase.

3) Location (optional)

If you enable location permission, the app uses your device’s location to calculate local UV information. You can deny or revoke this permission in Android settings and choose a city manually instead.

The latest device coordinates and cities you choose to save are stored locally in the app’s private storage so the app and widget can restore your location. They are not sent to us or to Firebase. You can remove this local information by clearing the app’s storage or uninstalling the app.

To produce the forecast, the app sends coordinates to MET Norway. They are deliberately rounded to approximately 0.01 degrees (about 1 km) to obscure your exact location, and are used only to retrieve temperature and cloud forecast data. Requests also reveal your IP address. MET Norway states that its API logs contain IP addresses and requested coordinates, and that these may be kept for up to 90 days. Recent forecast data is cached on your device to reduce repeated requests. Learn more: API terms and privacy policy.

Legal bases & regional details

EEA/UK/Switzerland (GDPR)

We use Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) to obtain consent for ad purposes when required. You’ll see a consent dialog on first launch in these regions. Your choice controls what data is used for advertising. To change your choice later, open the app → Settings → Privacy options.

Canada (PIPEDA and provincial privacy laws)

Location permission is optional and is used for the app’s UV features. App-usage analytics are not collected. Ad-related collection by Google is limited through the consent and privacy controls described above, where applicable.

United States (CCPA/CPRA)

We do not sell your personal information in the common sense of the word. For advertising, Google may process your device data to show ads; you can limit ad personalization at the system level (Android settings: Google → Ads → reset/delete Advertising ID).

Children’s privacy

This app is not directed to children under the age of digital consent. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information, please contact us and we will delete it.

Data sharing

We share data with Google AdMob solely to serve and measure ads. We send rounded coordinates to MET Norway solely to retrieve forecast data. See Google’s advertising policy pages for details on what Google collects and how you can control it: policies.google.com/technologies/ads. We do not sell personal data, and we do not share data with other third parties except as necessary for app functionality, advertising, compliance, or legal obligations.

Data storage & retention

We do not run our own backend for this app and do not retain personal data on our servers. Saved locations remain in the app’s private on-device storage until you replace them, clear the app’s storage, or uninstall the app. Ad data handled by Google follows Google’s retention policies. Weather forecast data may be cached locally on your device for a short time to improve performance and reduce repeated requests.

Security

We rely on platform security features (Android permissions, transport encryption) and reputable partners (Google AdMob and MET Norway). No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we strive to use commercially reasonable measures.

International transfers
